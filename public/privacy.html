Last updated: 2026-03-20

  • Account information (e.g., email address) to create and manage your account.
  • Authentication data provided by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Usage data (e.g., basic logs/diagnostics) to maintain and improve the Service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Service (login, account management, features).
  • To secure the Service and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate service-related messages (e.g., important changes).

3. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers used to run the Service (for example, hosting and authentication). We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your choices

You can access and update your account information within the app. You may also request deletion of your account where applicable.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: privacy@ecotwin.example.
